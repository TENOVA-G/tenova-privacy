1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Tenova Studios ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting player privacy while you enjoy TENOVA: Number Stack (the "Game"), our strategic number puzzle game on Android.

TENOVA is an offline-ready educational number puzzle game designed for players ages 6 and above. We do not require you to create an account, log in with social networks, or submit personal credentials. The Game can be played completely offline without internet connectivity.

2. Game Progress & Pure On-Device Play

TENOVA: Number Stack operates without requiring user accounts or registration. Player progress, high scores, move histories, unlocked character avatars, and booster inventories are processed and stored exclusively on your device.

  • Safe System-Generated Nicknames: Player nicknames (such as Nova4821, Titan7821, or Rex2048) are selected or generated automatically. There is no open text input field for player names in the game interface, preventing accidental submission of real names or sensitive personal data.
  • Local Milestone Progression: All 30 character avatars are unlocked through in-game high-score milestones. Unlocks are computed and stored exclusively on your device.
  • No Studio Server Collection: Tenova Studios does not operate backend servers that collect, synchronize, or receive your personal gameplay records.

3. Local Storage, Encryption, and Data Deletion

TENOVA stores gameplay progress locally on-device and disables Android cloud backup for its application data. Clearing TENOVA’s app storage permanently deletes all local game progress, encrypted records, and client settings.

  • Native Tink-Encrypted Records: Core gameplay records on the Android native filesystem are encrypted on-device using Google Tink AEAD (AES-256-GCM) with cryptographic master keys managed securely via the Android Keystore system. These encrypted records include:
    • All-time personal high scores (enc_best_score).
    • Active puzzle board state and undo/resume snapshots (enc_game_state, enc_game_state_backup).
    • Milestone-unlocked character avatars and selected avatar ID (enc_unlocked_avatars, enc_selected_avatar).
    • System-generated anonymous player nickname (enc_nickname).
    • Verified self-reported age category (enc_age_category), which Android uses to enforce age-based advertising protections.
    • Rewarded Hammer ad credits (enc_hammer_credits).
  • WebView Local Storage (localStorage): The internal WebView sandbox maintains unencrypted on-device browser storage for UI state and client-side gameplay functionality. Data stored in WebView localStorage is NOT encrypted with Tink AEAD and includes:
    • High Scores: Cached personal best score record (ns_merge10_best).
    • Achievements: Progress and completion timestamps for puzzle milestone achievement badges (tenova_achievements).
    • Hammer Credits: Client-side copy of rewarded Hammer ad progress and booster inventory (ns_hammer_credits, ns_starter_boosters_used).
    • Gameplay Preferences: Audio sound mute state (tenova_sound_muted) and tutorial completion flag (tenova_tutorial_completed).
    • Other Locally Stored Gameplay Information: Local profile caching (player ID, nickname, age category, avatar choices, score milestones) and local game statistics (merges, games played, booster use, ad reward nonces).
    Note: WebView localStorage does not determine the self-reported age category used for advertising. Native Android code authoritatively applies advertising eligibility rules using the encrypted native category. No real names, account passwords, or personal identity documents are stored in localStorage.
  • Cloud Backup Disabled: android:allowBackup="false" and android:fullBackupContent="false" are declared in the application manifest, preventing automated Android cloud backup extraction of local game records.
  • Data Deletion Scope & Mechanisms:
    1. Local Application Data Deletion: All local game records, Tink-encrypted high scores, save states, and WebView local storage exist solely on your physical device. You have direct and complete control over this data: selecting Clear Storage or Clear Data in Android Settings (under Apps > TENOVA > Storage), or uninstalling the application, permanently and irreversibly erases all local application data from your device.
    2. Support Communications: Emails sent voluntarily to Tenova Studios are kept while a support inquiry is active and for up to 90 days after resolution. Players and parents can request earlier deletion of their email communications by contacting tenova.studio.ds@gmail.com.
    3. Google-Managed Diagnostic & Ad Telemetry (External): Telemetry and technical diagnostic data collected directly by Google through Google Mobile Ads SDK or Google Play Services are transmitted directly to Google's servers. Tenova Studios does not operate backend servers, holds no copies of this telemetry, and has no technical access or administrative authority to inspect, modify, or delete records stored on Google's infrastructure. Deletion and retention of such data are governed strictly by Google's privacy policies and data retention schedules. Users can reset their App Set ID by uninstalling all applications published by Tenova Studios, or manage Google advertising and device settings via Android system settings.

4. Mandatory Age Screening & Advertising Protections (Code 21)

In Version 1.0.20 (Code 21), TENOVA enforces a mandatory, neutral age-screening process on first launch before gameplay or advertising features are accessible. The age input starts blank with no suggested, preselected, or default age, and cannot be skipped or dismissed by system back navigation.

TENOVA uses the resulting self-reported age category to configure Google Mobile Ads SDK (v25.5.0) and User Messaging Platform (UMP v4.0.0):

  • UNKNOWN Status (Unscreened): Prior to valid age submission or if the age record is missing, the profile is treated as UNKNOWN. In this state, advertising is permanently disabled, Google Mobile Ads SDK initialization is completely bypassed, and zero ad requests are made.
  • Child Profiles (Ages 0–12): For players who report an age under 13:
    • Configured with official AgeRestrictedTreatment.CHILD under COPPA child-directed treatment standards.
    • Maximum ad content rating is restricted to General Audiences (MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_G).
    • Non-personalized advertising is strictly enforced on all requests (npa=1).
    • Publisher privacy personalization is disabled (PublisherPrivacyPersonalizationState.DISABLED).
    • Publisher First-Party ID (also referred to as "same app key") opt-out is programmatically requested via MobileAds.putPublisherFirstPartyIdEnabled(false) immediately upon completion of SDK initialization. While TENOVA requests this opt-out at the earliest point permitted by the SDK API, TENOVA cannot guarantee that no internal identifier processing or transient initialization routines occur within the proprietary SDK prior to this callback.
    • In compliance with Google Play Families policy and COPPA, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent forms are completely bypassed for child profiles.
    • Native one-way latch: Once classified as CHILD, native Android architecture permanently blocks any subsequent age elevation attempt on the device.
  • Teen Profiles (Ages 13–17): For players between 13 and 17, under-age-of-consent protections (TFUA) are configured (AgeRestrictedTreatment.TEEN), content remains capped at MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_G, non-personalized advertising is requested, publisher personalization is disabled, and publisher first-party ID opt-out is requested after SDK initialization.
  • Adult Profiles (Ages 18+): Standard consent-governed advertising applies. Advertising initialization is strictly gated behind Google User Messaging Platform (UMP 4.0.0) signals; ad requests only proceed when UMP indicates platform conditions permit ad requests (canRequestAds == true).
  • Advertising ID Stripped: The com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and android.permission.ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID permissions are removed from the application manifest (tools:node="remove"). Google SDKs may still access or process identifiers as described below.
  • App Set ID Under Families Policy: Google Mobile Ads SDK may access an App Set ID scoped to the developer account. Under Google Play Families policy, permissible use cases for child audiences are restricted to documented non-advertising operational purposes, such as essential analytics and fraud prevention (never for ad personalization or behavioral tracking). Serving non-personalized advertisements does not automatically authorize identifier usage. While Google publishes policy guidance regarding identifier handling, the SDK's internal execution paths and whether Google's SDK uses App Set ID exclusively for permitted non-advertising purposes are proprietary to Google and cannot be independently inspected or confirmed by the application developer.
  • Voluntary Rewarded Booster Ads: Players may voluntarily watch rewarded advertisements to unlock extra booster credits (Hammer requires 2 completed ads; Swap requires 1 completed ad; Undo requires 1 completed ad; Revive provides center 3×3 clearance and a 500-point Supernova score bonus, capped at 1 use per game run). Rewarded ads are 100% optional, never required to play, and can be dismissed at any time without gameplay penalty.
  • Natural Break Retry Pacing & Dismissal: Interstitial ads are shown only at natural breaks (when tapping Retry after a game over), never during active puzzle gameplay or app launch. Pacing controls enforce a minimum 90-second cooldown between full-screen ads, show at most once every 3 completed retries, and cap at a maximum of 10 interstitial impressions per hour. Interstitials are suppressed if a rewarded ad was consumed in the same run. Sample interstitial advertisements were successfully dismissed during physical-device testing. This observation applies to the sample ads tested. Tenova Studios does not assert universal Families certification or guarantee third-party ad network behavior across all production ad fills, which are dynamically served by Google's ad systems.
  • Banner Ads Permanently Disabled: Banner ads are disabled (BANNER_ENABLED = false) to ensure an unhindered playing area with no touch interference.
  • Free Starter Boosters: At the start of each new game, child or unknown-age players receive two free Hammer uses. Other players receive one. All players receive one free Swap and one free Undo without watching advertisements.
